to steal ★

A scrappy field guide to the actions that pay you back in energy, instead of draining you dry.

CER stands for Compounding Energy Return. It asks one question. Does this hour, decision, or commitment pay back more usable energy than it costs? Four ledgers count. Body. Mind. Heart. Room.

25 Items · The Other Ledger Habits that pay you back in reverse

NCER. Negatively Compounding Energy Return. The cost is paid daily and stays small. The damage compounds quietly. Then a decade later it arrives all at once. The 100 above are the things to stack. These 25 are the things to actively cut.

NCER S · The 10 Worst Offenders
N1
Chronic sleep deprivation
Underwrites the decay of everything else. Brain, body, mood, judgement.
N2
Toxic long-term relationship
The single largest negative compounder available. Decades drain through it.
N3
Unmanaged chronic stress
Hormones, sleep, brain, body, all bleed at the same time.
N4
Sedentary as default
The modern killer. Compounds quietly into disease.
N5
Daily doomscrolling
Attention, mood, time, all leaking through the same hole.
N6
Heavy regular alcohol
Sleep, brain, gut, mood, relationships. All taxed daily.
N7
Smoking or vaping
Body and money, every day, for life. No upside.
N8
High-interest revolving debt
Capital compounding, running in reverse.
N9
Chronic negative self-talk
The internal narrator becomes destiny. Watch yours.
N10
Permanently avoiding hard conversations
Rot in slow motion. The conversation gets harder the longer you wait.

20s

The Foundation Decade.

Time is the cheap input here. Habits started now run the longest tail. The big mistake is treating health, friends, and money like they refill on their own. They do not.

Heaviest leverage anchors
Anchor 01
Craft skill investment
Pick one domain, get serious. Career capital compounds longest from here.
Anchor 02
Partner discernment
Learn to read character. The choice you make now shapes the next five decades.
Anchor 03
Savings rate, started early
A small percent saved now beats a large percent saved later. Time does the work.
Anchor 04
Baseline sleep and movement
Wire the habits before responsibilities pile up. Cheap now, expensive after.
Anchor 05
Read more than ever
Borrow minds while attention is cheap. Volume builds taste.
Anchor 06
Try widely on purpose
Low-stakes shots. Find the thread, before you pull it.
Anchor 07
Build a wide friend group
These people become the spine of the next forty years.
NCER risks for this decade
  • Treating health like it regenerates indefinitely
  • Sleep slides for work or social life
  • Debt accumulation as a default mode
  • Lifestyle creep tracking income
  • Choosing partners on chemistry alone
30s

The Consolidation Decade.

Compounding starts to show. The body sends its first signal that recovery is no longer free. Pick what to deepen and what to set down. Most 30-something energy crises come from refusing to drop the things that already paid out.

Heaviest leverage anchors
Anchor 01
Career compounding
Skill, reputation, network all start paying. Protect the runway.
Anchor 02
Partnership deepening
Dating becomes life-running. Maintenance is daily, not occasional.
Anchor 03
Structured strength training
Muscle loss starts now. Strength training stops being optional.
Anchor 04
Sleep quality
The body stops forgiving. Quality matters more than quantity.
Anchor 05
Parent time
The window is finite and closing faster than it feels.
Anchor 06
Purpose clarity
Stop exploring widely. Commit to the thread that proved itself.
Anchor 07
Financial discipline
Income rises, temptation rises. Lock savings as a percent, not a leftover.
NCER risks for this decade
  • Friendships erode under work and family load
  • Early signs of decline get ignored
  • Over-indexing on work at the cost of partnership
  • Sleep treated as negotiable
  • Stalling on purpose while waiting for certainty
40s

The Mastery and Maintenance Decade.

Career often peaks. Body asks for active maintenance, not just willingness. Parents need care. Partnership needs rekindling. Drift in this decade gets expensive fast.

Heaviest leverage anchors
Anchor 01
Strength training
Now critical. Bone density, muscle, balance, hormones all hinge on it.
Anchor 02
Sleep
Hormonal shifts hit sleep first. Protect it like a high-cost asset.
Anchor 03
Stress management
The body keeps score now. Recovery becomes non-negotiable.
Anchor 04
Marriage maintenance
Routine is the enemy. Rekindle on purpose. Repair fast.
Anchor 05
Parent care
Logistics increase. Be present for the harder conversations.
Anchor 06
Financial planning
Retirement, college, the unexpected. The math gets sharper now.
Anchor 07
Recommit to purpose
Old goals may have served. Pick new ones on purpose.
Anchor 08
Mentor others
Generativity feeds energy back. Teach what you know.
NCER risks for this decade
  • Midlife identity crisis driving rash moves
  • Body neglect masked by busyness
  • Financial decisions made under emotional weight
  • Coasting on past career capital
  • Marriage on auto-pilot
50s

The Transition Decade.

Body composition asks for aggressive management. Children leave. Career enters a new phase. Build the second half on purpose with everything earned in the first.

Heaviest leverage anchors
Anchor 01
Strength plus balance
Falls become real risks within a decade. Train balance now.
Anchor 02
Brain-engaging hobbies
New skills keep the brain plastic. Pick them up on purpose.
Anchor 03
Social network maintenance
The web thins by default. Tend it actively. Initiate, host, call.
Anchor 04
Hormone management
Get checked, get informed. Mood, sleep, body all depend on it.
Anchor 05
Retirement runway
Run the numbers. Make trade-offs explicit. Stop guessing.
Anchor 06
Mentor the next generation
Pass it on. Specifically, deliberately, with time.
Anchor 07
Process midlife meaning
Therapy, journaling, retreats. The interior work matters now.
NCER risks for this decade
  • Isolation after children leave or career peaks pass
  • Sedentary drift hidden by routine
  • Identity loss when an old role ends
  • Skipping health screenings
  • Coasting into a thin financial runway
60s

The Longevity Decade.

Compounding now favors maintenance. Mobility, social engagement, brain stimulation, and post-career purpose are the highest-CER moves. Keep them and you buy yourself more decade than most people expect.

Heaviest leverage anchors
Anchor 01
Balance and mobility
Fall prevention is the single highest-leverage practice. One bad fall erases a decade.
Anchor 02
Social engagement
Isolation raises mortality risk meaningfully. Stay in the room.
Anchor 03
Brain stimulation
Read, study, learn new things. Cognitive reserve is real.
Anchor 04
Post-career purpose
Volunteer, advise, write, teach. Work changes form but stays meaningful.
Anchor 05
Regular medical screening
Early detection turns a problem into a manageable thing.
Anchor 06
Intergenerational connection
Grandchildren, mentees, neighbors. Young company keeps the spirit young.
Anchor 07
Legacy work
Write the book. Tell the stories. Make what only you can make.
NCER risks for this decade
  • Sudden decline when maintenance lapses
  • Social withdrawal after retirement
  • Loss of structure once work ends
  • Postponing the legacy work indefinitely
  • Skipping medical screenings
